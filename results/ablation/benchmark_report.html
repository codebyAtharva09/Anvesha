4 · Methodology & metric definitions

Simulation
240 Hz physics, 30 Hz 640×480 camera (4°×3° base FOV), 60 Hz control, pan/tilt limited to 5 °/s; seeded PCG64 streams per subsystem (bit-reproducible).
Truth isolation
The tracker receives only frames, encoder angles, IMU rates and timestamps; ground truth is used only by the metrics engine.
Baselines
A threshold+CoG+PID, B blob+KF+PID, C CNN+KF+PID; all use spiral search; PID gains grid-tuned (best set kp 12, ki 20, kd 0.05).
Acquisition time
Run start → first frame in TRACK with true LOS error ≤ 10 px. "Acq ≤ 2 s %" counts seeds meeting this.
LOS / image error
Distance between beacon and boresight after acquisition; LOS excludes the white camera-jitter component (uncorrectable by a coarse gimbal), image error includes it.
Target loss
% of frames after acquisition with LOS error > 10 px or no lock, excluding scripted occlusion windows.
Re-acquisition
Per loss episode, from loss (or end of scripted occlusion) to the next on-target frame; unrecovered episodes counted separately.
Centroiding error
Raw per-frame centroid vs true sub-pixel beacon position (camera px).
FPS
Closed-loop rate including scene rendering, tracking and control on the machine above.

6 · Limitations & honest notes

  • All results are from simulation; no real camera or gimbal was used. The learned verifier is trained and evaluated on synthetic patches only.
  • Uniform ±20 px white jitter alone gives ≈16.3 px radial RMS image error — above 10 px for any coarse gimbal; a fine-steering stage is required.
  • Vibration above the gimbal bandwidth and very fast manoeuvres raise LOS error above 10 px; scripted occlusions add error while the beacon is invisible.
  • The all-maxima stress case (every PS disturbance at its maximum plus fog and look-alikes) is not solved.
